# co-Computational Diffie-Hellman

The CDH problem across the two groups of an asymmetric bilinear pairing: given $g_2, g_2^a \in \GG_2$ and $h \in \GG_1$, compute $h^a$; it is the assumption under which BLS signatures are EUF-CMA secure in the random oracle model.
