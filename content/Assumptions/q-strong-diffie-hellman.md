# $q$-Strong Diffie-Hellman assumption

Given the power sequence $(g, g^\tau, g^{\tau^2}, \ldots, g^{\tau^q})$ in a bilinear group with secret $\tau$, no efficient adversary can output a pair $(c, g^{1/(\tau + c)})$ with $c \in \ZZ_p$.
