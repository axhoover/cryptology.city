# k-LIN (disambiguation)

_$k$-LIN_ refers to one of the following.

- [$k$-Linear assumption](bilinear-map-assumptions#k-linear-assumption) ($k$-Lin) — a decisional assumption in a cyclic group; $k = 1$ is [[decisional-diffie-hellman|DDH]] and $k = 2$ is [[decisional-diffie-hellman#dlin|DLIN]].
- [Noisy $k$-LIN over expanders](noisy-k-lin-over-expanders) — distinguish $(\mathbf{M}, \mathbf{Ms}+\mathbf{e})$ from uniform for a sparse expanding matrix $\mathbf{M}$ over $\FF_p$ — [[GHJS25 - Public-Key Encryption from Planted Clique and Noisy k-LIN Over Expanders|GHJS25]].
- [[learning-parity-with-noise#Sparse Learning Parity with Noise|Sparse LPN]] — noisy $k$-LIN over $\FF_2$.
