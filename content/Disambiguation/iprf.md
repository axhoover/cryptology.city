# iPRF (disambiguation)

_iPRF_ refers to one of the following.

- [[pseudorandom-function#invertible-prfs|Invertible PRF]] — a PRF with an algorithm $\Invert(k, y)$ returning the preimage set of $y$ under $\Eval(k, \cdot)$.
- [[pseudorandom-function#pseudorandom-injective-functions|Injective PRF]] — a PRF $\Eval(k, \cdot)$ that is injective for every key $k$.
