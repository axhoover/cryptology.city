# Private information retrieval (disambiguation)

_PIR_ refers to one of the following.

- [[single-server-private-information-retrieval|Single-server PIR]] (cPIR) — one server holds the database; privacy is computational.
- [[multi-server-private-information-retrieval|Multi-server PIR]] (IT-PIR) — the database is replicated across non-colluding servers; privacy can be information-theoretic.
- [[doubly-efficient-pir|Doubly-efficient PIR]] (DEPIR) — single-server PIR with a preprocessed database and $o(n)$ query-time communication and computation.
- [[symmetric-private-information-retrieval-multi-server|Symmetric PIR]] (SPIR) — PIR that also hides from the client every database entry other than the one retrieved.
