# OPE (disambiguation)

_OPE_ refers to one of the following.

- [[order-preserving-encryption|Order-preserving encryption]] — deterministic symmetric encryption whose ciphertexts preserve the numerical order of the plaintexts.
- [[oblivious-polynomial-evaluation|Oblivious polynomial evaluation]] — a two-party functionality in which a receiver holding $\alpha$ learns $P(\alpha)$ for a sender's polynomial $P$ and nothing else.
