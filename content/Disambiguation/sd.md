# SD (disambiguation)

_SD_ refers to one of the following.

- [[syndrome-decoding|Syndrome decoding]] — given a uniform parity-check matrix $\mathbf{H} \in \FF_2^{(n-k) \times n}$ and the syndrome $\mathbf{H}\mathbf{e}$ of a uniform weight-$w$ vector $\mathbf{e}$, find a weight-$w$ vector with that syndrome.
- [[statistical-zero-knowledge#statistical-difference|Statistical Difference]] — the promise problem of deciding whether two circuit-sampled distributions are at total-variation distance at least $2/3$ or at most $1/3$.
- [[bilinear-map-assumptions#subgroup-decision|Subgroup decision]] — in a bilinear group of composite order $N = q_1 q_2$, distinguish a uniform element of the order-$q_1$ subgroup from a uniform group element.
