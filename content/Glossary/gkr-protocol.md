# GKR protocol

A public-coin interactive proof for any language computable by a log-space-uniform boolean circuit of depth $d$ on inputs of length $n$, with a polynomial-time prover, a verifier running in time $n \cdot \poly(d, \log n)$, and $\poly(d, \log n)$ communication.
