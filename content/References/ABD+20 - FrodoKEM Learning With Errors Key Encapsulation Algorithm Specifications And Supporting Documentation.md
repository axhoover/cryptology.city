# [ABD+20] FrodoKEM: Learning With Errors Key Encapsulation — Algorithm Specifications And Supporting Documentation

**Authors:** Erdem Alkim, Joppe W. Bos, Léo Ducas, Patrick Longa, Ilya Mironov, Michael Naehrig, Valeria Nikolaenko, Chris Peikert, Ananth Raghunathan, Douglas Stebila | **Venue:** NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization, Round 3 submission | [Source](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/post-quantum-cryptography/post-quantum-cryptography-standardization/round-3-submissions)

# Notes

The FrodoKEM specification of 30 September 2020, file `Supporting_Documentation/specification-round-3.pdf` of the FrodoKEM round-3 submission package; it has no abstract. Section 5.1 restates, with theorem numbers, the random-oracle theorems of [[HHK17 - A Modular Analysis of the Fujisaki-Okamoto Transformation|HHK17]] on the transforms T and U with implicit rejection (Lemmas 5.3 and 5.7, Theorem 5.1) and the quantum random-oracle theorem of [[JZC+18 - IND-CCA-Secure Key Encapsulation Mechanism in the Quantum Random Oracle Model, Revisited|JZC+18]] (Theorem 5.8).
