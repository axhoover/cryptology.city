# [Rob22] Breaking SIDH in Polynomial Time

**Authors:** Damien Robert | **Venue:** EUROCRYPT 2023 | [Source](https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/1038)

## Abstract

We show that we can break SIDH in classical polynomial time, even with a random starting curve $E_0$.
