# [BW07] Conjunctive, Subset, and Range Queries on Encrypted Data

**Authors:** Dan Boneh, Brent Waters | **Venue:** TCC 2007 | [Source](https://eprint.iacr.org/2006/287)

## Abstract

We construct public-key systems that support comparison queries $(x \geq a)$ on encrypted data as well as more general queries such as
subset queries $(x \in S)$. These systems also support arbitrary
conjunctive queries ($P_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge P_\ell$) without
leaking information on individual conjuncts. We present a general
framework for constructing and analyzing public-key systems
supporting queries on encrypted data.
