# [Duj04] Continued Fractions and RSA with Small Secret Exponent

**Authors:** Andrej Dujella | **Venue:** Tatra Mountains Mathematical Publications 2004 | [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0402052)

## Abstract

Extending the classical Legendre's result, we describe all solutions of the inequality $|x - a/b| < c/b^2$ in terms of convergents of continued fraction expansion of $x$. Namely, we show that $a/b = (rp_{m+1} \pm sp_m) / (rq_{m+1} \pm sq_m)$ for some nonnegative integers $m, r, s$ such that $rs < 2c$. As an application of this result, we describe a modification of Verheul and van Tilborg variant of Wiener's attack on RSA cryptosystem with small secret exponent.
