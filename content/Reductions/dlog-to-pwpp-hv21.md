# DLOG ∈ PWPP

## Statement

HV21 define a total search problem over groupoids given by circuits: an instance describes a binary operation, a generator $g$ and a target $h$, and a solution is either an exponent $x$ with $g^x = h$ or a witness that the instance does not describe a cyclic group generated by $g$. This problem is complete for [[total-function-np#pwpp|PWPP]], and a variant of it, Index, is complete for [[total-function-np#ppp|PPP]] — [[HV21 - On Search Complexity of Discrete Logarithm|HV21]]. On instances that are cyclic groups the only solutions are discrete logarithms, so [[discrete-logarithm|DLOG]] over cyclic groups given by circuits lies in PWPP; completeness transfers only to the groupoid problem, not to DLOG on genuine cyclic groups — [[HV21 - On Search Complexity of Discrete Logarithm|HV21]]. These answer an open question of [[SZZ18 - PPP-Completeness with Connections to Cryptography|SZZ18]].

## Notes

- Membership goes through a reduction to the problem General-Claw — [[HV21 - On Search Complexity of Discrete Logarithm|HV21]]. The direct collision argument through $(x, y) \mapsto g^x h^y$ ([[dlog-to-crhf-cvhp91|DLOG ⇒ CRHF]]) extracts $\log_g h$ only in groups of prime order. In the group of order $6$ generated by $g = 3$ modulo $7$, with $h = g^2 = 2$, the pairs $(0, 0)$ and $(0, 3)$ collide, but they give only $3 \log_g h \equiv 0 \pmod 6$, which $0$, $2$ and $4$ all satisfy — standard.
- Average-case hardness of other TFNP subclasses, such as PPAD, from discrete logarithms would need structure beyond what collision resistance provides — [[HV21 - On Search Complexity of Discrete Logarithm|HV21]].
