# Sub-exponential DDH ⇒ NIZK argument for NP

## Statement

If [[decisional-diffie-hellman#sub-exponential-ddh|DDH is sub-exponentially hard]] for some group generator (no pairing is needed), every $\classNP$ language has a [[non-interactive-zero-knowledge#nizk-arguments-for-np|NIZK argument]] in the common reference string model with adaptive soundness and computational zero knowledge — [[JJ21 - Non-interactive Zero Knowledge from Sub-exponential DDH|JJ21]]. The proof needs DDH hardness only against polynomial-time adversaries, with sub-exponentially small advantage. It instantiates Fiat–Shamir with a correlation-intractable hash family for constant-depth threshold circuits, built from a new tool, interactive trapdoor hashing protocols — [[JJ21 - Non-interactive Zero Knowledge from Sub-exponential DDH|JJ21]].

## Notes

- The main construction of the same paper is a NIZK argument in the common random string model with adaptive multi-theorem statistical zero knowledge and only non-adaptive soundness; relaxing zero knowledge to computational is what gives adaptive soundness — [[JJ21 - Non-interactive Zero Knowledge from Sub-exponential DDH|JJ21]].
- The paper also gives zap arguments for $\classNP$ under the same assumption. Their soundness is computational, so they are not [[zero-knowledge-proof#zaps|zaps]] in the Dwork–Naor sense, which are proofs — [[JJ21 - Non-interactive Zero Knowledge from Sub-exponential DDH|JJ21]].
- In groups with a symmetric bilinear pairing, NIZK proofs for $\classNP$ follow from DLIN: [[bdh-to-nizk-gro16|DLIN ⇒ NIZK]] — [[GOS06 - Non-interactive Zaps and New Techniques for NIZK|GOS06b]].
