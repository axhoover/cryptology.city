# PPP ⊆ TFNP

## Statement

Every problem in [[total-function-np#ppp|PPP]] has a solution on every input, guaranteed by the combinatorial principle defining the class, and solutions are verifiable in polynomial time, so PPP $\subseteq$ [[total-function-np|TFNP]] — folklore.
