# DLOG ⊆ TFNP

## Statement

For a group generator $\GrGen$ for which [[discrete-logarithm#dlog-in-verifiable-prime-order-groups|DLOG is hard in verifiable prime-order groups]], the [[discrete-logarithm|DLOG]] search problem of finding $x$ with $g^x = h$ given $(\GG, g, p, h)$ is in [[total-function-np|TFNP]] — folklore. The order-$p$ subgroup of $\ZZ_P^*$ for primes $p$ and $P$ with $p \mid P - 1$ is an example: since $\ZZ_P^*$ is cyclic, $h^p \equiv 1 \pmod P$ decides membership of $h \in [1, P - 1]$ in it, and every element of it other than $1$ generates it — standard.

## Sketch

The verifier first runs the decider for verifiable prime-order groups on its input, which accepts $(\GG, g, p, h)$ exactly when $\GG$ is a group of prime order $p$ generated by $g$ and $h \in \GG \setminus \{1\}$. On an accepted input $h$ has a discrete logarithm $x \in \ZZ_p$, and a candidate $x$ is checked with one exponentiation; on a rejected input the verifier accepts a fixed dummy solution, and $h = 1$ has the solution $0$ anyway.

## Notes

- Suitable formulations of discrete logarithm over general groups are complete for PPP and PWPP, the pigeonhole subclasses of TFNP, answering an open question of [[SZZ18 - PPP-Completeness with Connections to Cryptography|SZZ18]] — [[HV21 - On Search Complexity of Discrete Logarithm|HV21]].
