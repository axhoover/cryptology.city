# PPA ⊆ TFNP

## Statement

Every problem in [[total-function-np#ppa|PPA]] has a solution on every input, guaranteed by the combinatorial principle defining the class, and solutions are verifiable in polynomial time, so PPA $\subseteq$ [[total-function-np|TFNP]] — folklore.
