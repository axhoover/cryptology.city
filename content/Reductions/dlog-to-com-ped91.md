# DLOG ⇒ Statistically hiding commitment

## Statement

For $(\GG, g, p) \gets \GrGen(1^\secpar)$ with $\GG$ of prime order $p$ and $h \getsr \GG \setminus \{1\}$, the Pedersen commitment to $m \in \ZZ_p = \{0, \ldots, p-1\}$ is $c = g^m h^r$ for $r \getsr \ZZ_p$, opened by revealing $(m, r) \in \ZZ_p \times \ZZ_p$, which always verifies; its commitment and decommitment spaces are $\GG \cup \{\bot\}$ and $\ZZ_p^2 \cup \{\bot\}$. It is [[commitment-scheme#statistically-hiding-commitments|perfectly hiding]], also for a receiver-chosen $\pp$, since $\Com$ first checks that $\GG$ is a group of prime order $p$ generated by $g$ and that $h \in \GG \setminus \{1\}$ and outputs $(\bot, \bot)$ if not (for the order-$p$ subgroup of $\ZZ_P^*$ with $P = 2p + 1$ prime: $P$ and $p$ prime, $g \not\equiv 1$, $h \not\equiv 1$ and $g^p \equiv h^p \equiv 1 \pmod P$), and computationally binding if [[discrete-logarithm#dlog-in-verifiable-prime-order-groups|DLOG]] is hard for $\GrGen$, since two openings $(m, r) \ne (m', r')$ of one $c$ give $\log_g h = (m - m')(r' - r)^{-1} \bmod p$ — [[Ped91 - Non-Interactive and Information-Theoretic Secure Verifiable Secret Sharing|Ped91b]].

## Sketch

Hiding: on every $\pp$ that passes the check, $h$ generates $\GG$, so $h^r$ is uniform over $r \getsr \ZZ_p$ and $c$ is uniform, independent of $m$; on a $\pp$ that fails it, $\Com$ outputs $(\bot, \bot)$ whatever the message. Binding: on DLOG challenge $X$ the reduction outputs $0$ if $X = 1$, and otherwise sets $h = X$, runs the binding adversary and turns its two openings into $\log_g X$.

```pseudocode
\begin{algorithm}
\algname{Algorithm}
\caption{$\Gen(1^\secpar)$}
\begin{algorithmic}
\State $(\GG, g, p) \gets \GrGen(1^\secpar)$; $h \getsr \GG \setminus \{1\}$
\Return $\pp \gets (\GG, g, p, h)$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}
\algname{Algorithm}
\caption{$\Com(\pp, m; r)$}
\begin{algorithmic}
\If{$\GG$ is not a group of prime order $p$ generated by $g$, or $h \notin \GG \setminus \{1\}$}
\Return $(c, d) \gets (\bot, \bot)$
\EndIf
\Return $(c, d) \gets (g^m h^r, (m, r))$
\Comment{$m, r \in \ZZ_p$}
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}
\algname{Algorithm}
\caption{$\Open(\pp, c, d)$}
\begin{algorithmic}
\If{$d = (m, r)$ with $m, r \in \ZZ_p$, and $c = g^m h^r$}
\Return $m$
\EndIf
\Return $\bot$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
```
